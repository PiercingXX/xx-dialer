XX-Phone

The phone app that knows who is allowed to ring. A default dialer for GrapheneOS with a ring-policy engine: spam never rings, starred always rings, everyone else rings only inside their window — unknown numbers on their own ringtone, 9 to 5.

01 · The ring policy

First match wins, and the order is printed in the app

This table renders verbatim on the Rules screen. The precedence is the documentation.

ConditionVerdictTone
1Emergency callback windowRingdefault
2Contact set to Send to voicemail→ voicemail—
3Blocklist or pattern rule✗ Block—
4STIR/SHAKEN failed — forged caller ID✗ Block—
5★ Starred contactRing · any timedefault
6Business contact, 09:00–19:00Ringdefault
7Business contact, outside window→ Silence—
8Saved contactRing · any timedefault
9Unknown you dialed in the last 48 hRing · any timeunknown tone
10Silence pattern (neighbor-spoof preset)→ Silence—
11Unknown, 09:00–17:00Ringunknown tone
12Unknown, outside window→ Silence—

Overrides: a repeat caller (15 min) pierces any Silence, never a Block. Expecting a call turns every Silence into a ring for a bounded 2 h, then expires on its own. In observe mode — the first-week default — every call rings normally while the log records what would have happened.

Silence means surfaced, not vanished: the call lands in Recents with the rule that fired, still answerable while it rings out. Blocks come only from explicit signals — a heuristic can silence a call, never eat one. Every failure path resolves to the phone rings.

02 · Screens

Four tabs, two call surfaces, one setup flow

Views + viewBinding. Monochrome plus the one Signal-white accent — the Answer block is the accent.

Reserved white

Body text caps at 90%. The Answer block, the active tab, and the ★ are the Signal moments — the accent is inversion, not hue.

Glyph-first status

✓ rang · → silenced · ✗ blocked. Only ✗ may use error red — a blocked call is the one loud state. Spam gets no orange badge; the absence of a ring is the feature.

Tabular everything

Keypad digits, timers, timestamps — Space Mono, tabular figures. A reflowing call timer is the tell of an unconsidered dialer.

Answer interaction

Two-button tap by default; horizontal pill slider as the setting. Vertical swipe is the gesture Google publicly abandoned as error-prone.

Observe first

The first week silences nothing: every call rings while the log records what would have happened. A phone app earns trust before it's allowed to eat a ring — and enforcement is offered, never flipped.

Recovery on the notification

A silenced call's quiet card carries Call back · Ring next time · Block inline. Bursts batch ("3 silenced calls"); the whole category is set to immediately, daily summary, or never.
